    About

    Created and hosted by JML (2026) for use at Hoppers Crossing Secondary College.

    How to Race
    1. Type your name and join. The host starts the match from their screen.
    2. Each round you are paired with someone new. With an odd number of players, one group has three.
    3. After a 3-second countdown every group gets the same cards: 4 or 5, chosen by the host. Use every card to make 24.
    4. Click a card, an operation, then another card to combine them. Cards stay whole numbers.
    5. When your last card is 24 you score points. Everyone in your group can score, and the first to finish wins the race. A round lasts at most 30 seconds.

    Points: making 24 scores 1,000 points for an instant answer, dropping steadily to 500 at 30 seconds. Finishing first in your group adds a 100-point bonus. Everyone's points go into one scoreboard.

    Stuck? Give Up ends your round; your opponents can keep going.
