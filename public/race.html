    How to Race
    1. Both players join and press I'm Ready.
    2. After a 3-second countdown you both get the same four cards.
    3. Click a card, an operation, then another card to combine them. Cards stay whole numbers.
    4. The first player whose last card is 24 wins the round.
    5. A match is 1 to 10 rounds (the first player to join chooses). The scoreboard after each round shows who is winning.

    Points: winning a round scores up to 1,000 points. The faster you solve it, the more you score, and every win is worth at least 500.

    Stuck? Give Up lets your opponent keep going. If you both give up, the answer is shown.
