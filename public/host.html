    About

    Created and hosted by JML (2026) for use at Hoppers Crossing Secondary College.

    How Hosting Works
    1. Show this screen on the projector. Players open the address shown and type their names.
    2. Choose the match length and press Start Match.
    3. Each round, players are drawn into pairs (one group of three when there's an odd number) and race the same cards (4 or 5, your choice). A round lasts at most 30 seconds.
    4. Everyone who makes 24 scores 1,000 points for an instant answer, dropping to 500 at 30 seconds. The first in each group gets 100 bonus points.
    5. Once the match starts the lobby is closed: new players can’t join, but anyone who drops out can rejoin with the same name and keeps their score.
    6. Watch each race and the live scoreboard here. After each round's results, the next round starts by itself after a 3-second countdown.

    Names: in the waiting room, click ✎ next to a player's name to change it.

    Bonk: if you suspect someone is cheating, press Bonk next to their name on the scoreboard to take 500 points from them (never below 0). Everyone sees their row shake with a big “BONK!”.

    End Game stops the match at any point. Races still going stop, points already scored count, and the scoreboard at that moment is the final result.

    The host screen doesn't play. Only one host screen can be open at a time.
