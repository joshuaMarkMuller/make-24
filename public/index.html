How to Play

Goal: use all four cards and + − × ÷ to make exactly 24.

  1. Click a card, then an operation, then another card.
  2. The two cards combine into one new card.
  3. Keep going until one card is left. If it says 24, you win!

Cards are always whole numbers, so ÷ only works when it divides exactly, and − can't go below zero. Every puzzle can be solved. Some need brackets: combining two cards first works like brackets, so 8 − 2 and then × 4 is (8 − 2) × 4.

Keyboard: 1–4 pick a card · + − * / pick an operation · Backspace undo · R reset · S reveal · F2 or N new puzzle
